# Curriculum research and pre-implementation alignment matrix

> Historical 20 October 2026 sample alignment work. The current 2025/2026 Marathi textbooks were researched on 23 October 2026 in [the gated Stage 1 report](../stage1/STAGE1_REPORT.md). The 56-row matrix below is **not** the approved current curriculum and must not feed Stage 2 without that review.

Research: 20 October 2026. The CSV was created before implementing new content. All wording is original and learning-outcome IDs are application IDs, never official codes.

Consulted official sources:
- https://maa.ac.in/english/index.php?tcf=learningoutcomes — English booklet and Standards 1–5 PDF links returned 404. Exact outcome-code mapping cannot be verified.
- https://maa.ac.in/english/index.php?tcf=curriculum — framework, foundational curriculum and teacher-handbook listings; overview is not a topic-by-class syllabus.
- https://ebalbharati.in/ — official textbook portal.
- https://books.ebalbharati.in/ebookmwb.aspx — official Class 1–4 workbook downloads. Downloaded scans to temporary research storage; no textbook pages are shipped with the app.
- Official workbook PDF page 4 (all four classes): teacher guidance emphasises practice aligned to teaching and learning, allowing learners to work, observing difficulty and providing support. This informs short guided practice and contextual help.
- Class 1 PDF page 5: comparative size words; not yet covered as a dedicated activity.
- Class 2 PDF page 5: numeral/word recognition and number sequence practice; topic corroborated only.
- Class 3 PDF page 5: tens/ones and digit place value; topic corroborated only.
- Class 4 PDF page 5: numerals in two scripts and larger number names; topic corroborated only. Current generator covers a smaller foundational range.

The scanned workbook editions are older and do not establish current curriculum completeness. All 56 rows therefore retain **needs curriculum review**. Three rows have observed topic evidence, not official approval. No complete textbook pages, images, or question banks are copied.

Class plans and recommended ordering are proposed teaching progressions, not official syllabus claims. Each class/topic record includes objective, prerequisite, vocabulary, bilingual definition, hint, common mistake and reference. Examples are computed and validated at runtime. Curriculum review must confirm grade ranges, current chapters and language with a Maharashtra primary teacher.
